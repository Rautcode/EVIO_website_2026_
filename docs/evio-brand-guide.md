# EVIO Brand Guide & Tokens

## 🎨 Color Palette
- **Primary Background (Dark mode / Hero):** `#0A1628` (Deep Navy)
- **Primary Accent (Buttons, Highlights, Icons):** `#00AEEF` (Electric Blue)
- **Secondary Accent (Hover states, Energy feel):** `#F59E0B` (Amber/Orange)
- **Text on Dark:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)
- **Text on Light:** `#1A202C` (Near Black)
- **Section Background Light:** `#F4F6F9` (Soft Gray)
- **Card Background:** `#FFFFFF` with box-shadow: `0 4px 24px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)`
- **Border/Divider:** `#E2E8F0`

## 🖋️ Typography
- **Headlines:** Syne Bold (700 weight)
- **Body Text:** Inter Regular and Medium (400, 500)
- **Desktop Hero Headline:** 72px Syne Bold
- **Mobile Hero Headline:** 40px Syne Bold
- **Section Headline (H2):** 48px Desktop / 32px Mobile
- **Sub-headline (H3):** 32px Desktop / 24px Mobile
- **Body Paragraph:** 16px Inter, line-height 1.7
- **Label / Tag:** 13px Inter Medium, uppercase, letter-spacing 0.08em

## 📏 Spacing & Layout Rules
- **Max Content Width:** 1280px (Centered)
- **Section Padding:** 100px y-axis (desktop) / 60px y-axis (mobile)
- **Grid Gap:** 24px
- **Card Border Radius:** 16px
- **Button Border Radius:** 8px (or full pill)

## 🔘 Button Styles
- **Primary Button:** Electric Blue Background, White Text, 16px font, 48px height, 24px horizontal padding, 8px radius.
- **Secondary Button:** Transparent background, Electric Blue border (2px), Electric Blue Text.
- **Ghost Button (on dark backgrounds):** White Border, White Text (becomes solid blue on hover).
